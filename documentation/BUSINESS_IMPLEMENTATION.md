# Business Implementation Guide

## 🎯 **Business Model Overview**

### Value Proposition
MyGETS transforms procurement from a tactical function into a strategic advantage by providing the first OCDS-native platform that automatically structures all procurement data according to international standards, enabling enhanced transparency, compliance, and strategic insights.

### Target Market
- **Primary**: Government agencies and regulated industries
- **Secondary**: Enterprise organizations seeking procurement optimization
- **Tertiary**: Procurement consultants and service providers

### Revenue Model
- **SaaS Subscription**: Tiered pricing based on organization size and features
- **Early Adopter Program**: Preferential pricing for initial customers
- **Implementation Services**: Professional services for setup and integration
- **Training and Support**: Premium support and training packages

- **Price**: $1,500/month ($15,000 annually with 17% discount)
- **Target**: Small to medium organizations
- **Features**:
  - Core OCDS-compliant procurement platform
  - Up to 10 user accounts
  - Standard reporting and analytics
  - Email support during business hours
  - 10GB document storage
  - Basic integrations

#### Professional Tier
- **Price**: $3,000/month ($30,000 annually with 17% discount)
- **Target**: Medium to large organizations
- **Features**:
  - All Standard features
  - Up to 25 user accounts
  - Advanced analytics and custom dashboards
  - Priority email and phone support
  - 50GB document storage
  - API access for custom integrations
  - AI-powered document analysis
  - Supplier portal access

#### Enterprise Tier
