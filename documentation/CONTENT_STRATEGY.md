# Content Strategy & Implementation

## 🎯 **Content Overview**

### Content Philosophy
MyGETS content is built around three core principles:
1. **Educational Value**: Making OCDS and procurement concepts accessible
2. **Persona-Specific**: Tailored messaging for different user types
3. **Action-Oriented**: Clear calls-to-action and conversion paths

### Content Pillars
- **OCDS Education**: Comprehensive understanding of Open Contracting Data Standard
- **Platform Benefits**: Clear value proposition and feature explanations
- **Early Adopter Program**: Exclusive benefits and application process
- **Industry Solutions**: Targeted solutions for different sectors
