### 1. Google Search Console Submission

#### Step 1: Verify Ownership
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)
2. Add your property: `https://mygets.net`
3. Choose verification method:
   - **HTML file**: Upload verification file to `/public/`
   - **HTML tag**: Add meta tag to `<head>` section
   - **DNS record**: Add TXT record to domain DNS

#### Step 2: Submit Sitemap
1. In Search Console, go to **Sitemaps** section
2. Enter: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**
4. Monitor indexing status

### 2. Bing Webmaster Tools Submission

#### Step 1: Verify Ownership
1. Go to [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)
2. Add your site: `https://mygets.net`
3. Verify ownership using similar methods as Google

#### Step 2: Submit Sitemap
1. Go to **Sitemaps** section
2. Add: `https://mygets.net/sitemap.xml`
3. Submit and monitor
